![[20260819001]]
**方法1：定义法**
设圆的半径为$2a,|OA|=2c,c<a$
以$OA$的中点为原点，$OA$所在的直线为$x$轴建立平面直角坐标系
连接$AP,$易得$|PM|=|PA|$
$\therefore|PO|+|PA|=|PM|=2a$
$\therefore P$在以$O,A$为焦点的椭圆上
此时轨迹方程为$\frac{x^2}{a^2}+\frac{y^2}{b^2}=1$
**方法2：交轨法**
点$O(-c,0),A(c,0)$
设点$M(x_0,y_0)$ ,则$(x_0+c)^2+y_0^2=4a^2$
$OM$的方程为$$y=\frac{y_0}{x_0+c}(x+c)$$
直线$AM$的斜率为$$k=\frac{y_0}{x_0-c}$$
$AM$中垂线的方程为$$y-\frac{y_0}{2}=-\frac{x_0-c}{y_0}(x-\frac{x_0+c}{2})$$
即$$y=-\frac{x_0-c}{y_0}x+\frac{x_0^2-c^2+y_0^2}{2y_0}=-\frac{x_0-c}{y_0}(x-c)+\frac{4a^2-2cx_0-2xc^2}{2y_0}$$
联立方程$$\begin{cases}y=\frac{y_0}{x_0+c}(x+c)\\y=-\frac{x_0-c}{y_0}x+\frac{4a^2-c^2-2cx_0}{2y_0}\end{cases}\Rightarrow \begin{cases}yx_0-(x+c)y_0=-cy\\(x+c)x_0+yy_0=2a^2-c^2-cx_0+cx\end{cases}$$
解得$$y_0=\frac{2y(a^2+cx)}{(x+c)^2+y^2}\cdots①$$
另外$$\begin{cases}y=\frac{y_0}{x_0+c}(x+c)\\(x_0+c)^2+y_0^2=4a^2\end{cases}\Rightarrow \begin{cases}(x_0+c)y=y_0(x+c)\\(x_0+c)^2+y_0^2=4a^2\end{cases}$$
则有$$(4a^2-y_0^2)y^2=y_0^2(x+c)^2\Rightarrow y_0^2=\frac{4a^2y^2}{(x+c)^2+y^2}$$
将①代入上式整理得$$\frac{x^2}{a^2}+\frac{y^2}{a^2-c^2}=1$$
