已知椭圆的方程$\frac{x^2}{4}+\frac{y^2}{3}=1$左焦点为$F$,右顶点为$A$,直线$l:x=-4$,过$F$作直线交椭圆于$M,N,AM$交$l$于点$P，AN$交$l$于点$Q$,线段$PQ$的中点为$R$，证明$RF\perp MN$
#### 设直线MN

设直线$MN$的方程为$x=ty-1,M(x_1,y_1),N(x_2,y_2)$
代入椭圆方程整理得$$(3t^2+4)y^2-6ty-9=0$$
$$y_1+y_2=\frac{6t}{3t^2+4},y_1y_2=\frac{-9}{2t^2+4}$$
$A,M,P$共线得$$\frac{y_{P}}{-4-2}=\frac{y_1}{x_1-2}\Rightarrow,y_p=\frac{-6y_1}{x_1-2}\Rightarrow P(-4,\frac{-6y_1}{x_1-2})$$
同理得$$Q(-4,\frac{-6y_2}{x_1-2})$$
所以$$y_R=-3(\frac{y_1}{x_1-2}+\frac{y_2}{x_2-2})\\=-3\times\frac{y_1(ty_2-3)+y_2(ty_1-3)}{(ty_1-3)(ty_2-3)}\\=-3\frac{2ty_1y_2-3(y_1+y_2)}{t^2y_1y_2-3t(y_1+y_2)+36}=3t$$
$$R(-4,3t)$$
$$k_{FR}=\frac{3t}{-3}=-t,k_{MN}=\frac{1}{t}\Rightarrow FR\perp MN$$

#### 设直线AP、AQ
设直线$AP$的方程为$x=t_1y+2$
$AQ$的方程为$x=t_2y+2$
联立方程$$\begin{cases}x=t_1y+2\\3x^2+4y^2-12=0\end{cases}$$
整理得$$(3t_1^2+4)y^2+12t_1y=0\Rightarrow y_1=\frac{-12t_1}{3t_1^2+4}$$
$$M(\frac{8-6t_1^2}{3t_1^2+4},\frac{-12t_1}{3t_1^2+4})$$
同理$$N(\frac{8-6t_2^2}{3t_2^2+4},\frac{-12t_2}{3t_2^2+4})$$
$M,N,F$共线得$$\frac{-12t_1}{12-3t_1^2}=\frac{-12t_2}{12-3t_2^2}$$
$$t_1(12-3t_2^2)=t_2(12-3t_1^2)\Rightarrow t_1t_2=-4$$
$$k_{RF}=\frac{t_1+t_2}{t_1t_2}=-\frac{t_1+t_2}{4}$$
$$k_{MN}=\frac{-12t_1(3t_2^2+4)+12t_2(3t_1^2+4)}{(8-6t_1^2)(3t_2^2+4)-(8-6t_2^2)(3t_1^2+4)}=\frac{12(t_1-t_2)(3t_1t_2-4)}{48t_2^2-48t_1^2}=\frac{4}{t_1+t_2}$$
$$k_{RF}\cdot k_{MN}=-1$$
####  设点P、Q
设点$P(-4,m),Q(-4,n)$
直线$$AP:x=\frac{-6}{m}y+2$$
直线$$AQ:x=\frac{-6}{n}y+2$$
$$\begin{cases}x=\frac{-6}{m}y+2\\3x^2+4y^2-12=0\end{cases}$$
整理得
$$y=\frac{18m}{m^2+27}$$
从而点$$M(\frac{2m^2-54}{m^2+27},\frac{18m}{m^2+27})$$
同理$$N(\frac{2n^2-54}{n^2+27},\frac{18n}{n^2+27})$$
点$M,N,F$共线
所以$$\frac{18m}{3m^2-27}=\frac{18n}{3n^2-27}\Rightarrow3mn^2-9m=m^2n-9n\Rightarrow mn=-9$$
$$k_{RF}=-\frac{m+n}{6}$$
$$k_{MN}=\frac{18m(n^2+27)-18n(m^2+27)}{(2m^2-54)(n^2+27)-(2n^2-54)(m^2+27)}=\frac{18(n-m)(mn-27)}{108m^2-108n^2}=\frac{6}{m+n}$$
$$\therefore FR\perp MN$$
