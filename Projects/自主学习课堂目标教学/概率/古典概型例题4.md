在两名男生（$B_1$和$B_2$）和两名女生($G_1$和$G_2$)中抽取两人
(1)分别写出不放回简单随机抽样，有放回简单随机抽样和按性别分层抽样的样本空间
(2)在三种抽样方式下，分别求抽到两人都是男生的概率

解：设第一次抽取的人记为 $x_{1}$ ，第二次抽取的人记为 $x_{2}$ ，则可用数组 $(x_{1}, x_{2})$ 表示样本点.

  

(1) 根据相应的抽样方法可知：

  

有放回简单随机抽样的样本空间

  

$$

\begin{array}{l} \Omega_ {1} = \left\{\left(\mathrm{B} _ {1}, \mathrm{B} _ {1}\right), \left(\mathrm{B} _ {1}, \mathrm{B} _ {2}\right), \left(\mathrm{B} _ {1}, \mathrm{G} _ {1}\right), \left(\mathrm{B} _ {1}, \mathrm{G} _ {2}\right), \left(\mathrm{B} _ {2}, \mathrm{B} _ {1}\right), \left(\mathrm{B} _ {2}, \mathrm{B} _ {2}\right), \left(\mathrm{B} _ {2}, \mathrm{B} _ {1}\right) \right. \\ \left. \mathrm{G} _ {1}\right), \left(\mathrm{B} _ {1}, \mathrm{G} _ {2}\right), \left(\mathrm{G} _ {1}, \mathrm{B} _ {1}\right), \left(\mathrm{G} _ {1}, \mathrm{B} _ {2}\right), \left(\mathrm{G} _ {1}, \mathrm{G} _ {1}\right), \left(\mathrm{G} _ {1}, \mathrm{G} _ {2}\right), \left(\mathrm{G} _ {2}, \mathrm{B} _ {1}\right), \\ \left. \left(\mathrm{G} _ {2}, \mathrm{B} _ {2}\right), \left(\mathrm{G} _ {2}, \mathrm{G} _ {1}\right), \left(\mathrm{G} _ {2}, \mathrm{G} _ {2}\right) \right\rbrace , \\ \end{array}

$$

  

不放回简单随机抽样的样本空间

  

$$

\Omega_ {2} = \left(\left(\mathrm{B} _ {1}, \mathrm{B} _ {2}\right), \left(\mathrm{B} _ {1}, \mathrm{G} _ {1}\right), \left(\mathrm{B} _ {1}, \mathrm{G} _ {2}\right), \left(\mathrm{B} _ {2}, \mathrm{B} _ {1}\right), \left(\mathrm{B} _ {2}, \mathrm{G} _ {1}\right), \left(\mathrm{B} _ {2}, \mathrm{G} _ {2}\right), \left(\mathrm{G} _ {1}, \right. \right.

$$

  

$$

\left. \mathrm{B} _ {1}\right), \left(\mathrm{G} _ {1}, \mathrm{B} _ {2}\right), \left(\mathrm{G} _ {1}, \mathrm{G} _ {2}\right), \left(\mathrm{G} _ {2}, \mathrm{B} _ {1}\right), \left(\mathrm{G} _ {2}, \mathrm{B} _ {2}\right), \left(\mathrm{G} _ {2}, \mathrm{G} _ {1}\right) \rangle .

$$

  

按性别等比例分层随机抽样，先从男生中抽一人，再从女生中抽一人，其样本空间

  

$$

\Omega_ {3} = \left\{\left(\mathrm{B} _ {1}, \mathrm{G} _ {1}\right), \left(\mathrm{B} _ {1}, \mathrm{G} _ {2}\right), \left(\mathrm{B} _ {2}, \mathrm{G} _ {1}\right), \left(\mathrm{B} _ {2}, \mathrm{G} _ {2}\right) \right\}.

$$

  

(2) 设事件 A = “抽到两名男生”，则

  

对于有放回简单随机抽样，

  

$$

A = \left\{\left(\mathrm{B} _ {1}, \mathrm{B} _ {1}\right), \left(\mathrm{B} _ {1}, \mathrm{B} _ {2}\right), \left(\mathrm{B} _ {2}, \mathrm{B} _ {1}\right), \left(\mathrm{B} _ {2}, \mathrm{B} _ {2}\right) \right\}.

$$

  

因为抽中样本空间 $\Omega_{1}$ 中每一个样本点的可能性都相等，所以这是一个古典概型.因此

  

$$

P (A) = \frac {4}{1 6} = \frac {1}{4}.

$$

  

对于不放回简单随机抽样，

  

$$

A = \left\{\left(B _ {1}, B _ {2}\right), \left(B _ {2}, B _ {3}\right) \right\}.

$$

  

因为抽中样本空间 $\Omega_{2}$ 中每一个样本点的可能性都相等，所以这是一个古典概型。因此

  

$$

P (A) = \frac {2}{1 2} = \frac {1}{6}.

$$

  

因为按性别等比例分层随机抽样，不可能抽到两名男生，所以 $A = \emptyset$ ，因此 $P(A) = 0$
