已知$\triangle ABC$中，$\angle A,\angle B,\angle C$的对边分别为$a,b,c,A=B+2C,b+c=1$,求$a$的最小值
【解析】
$\frac{a}{\sin A}=\frac{b}{\sin B}=\frac{c}{\sin C}$可得
$\frac{a}{\sin A}=\frac{b+c}{\sin B+\sin C}$
$\therefore a=\frac{\sin A}{\sin B+\sin C}$
$\because A+B+C=\pi$
$A=B+2C,B=A-2C$
可得
$B=\frac{\pi-3C}{2},A=\frac{\pi+C}{2}$
$\therefore \sin A=\cos \frac{C}{2},\sin B=\cos\frac{3C}{2}$
$a=\frac{\cos\frac{C}{2}}{\cos\frac{3C}{2}+\sin C}$
统一变量到$\frac{C}{2}$
由三倍角余弦公式
$\frac{1}{a}=\frac{4\cos^3\frac{C}{2}-3\cos C+2\sin\frac{C}{2}\cos\frac{C}{2}}{\cos \frac{C}{2}}$
$=4\cos^2\frac{C}{2}+2\sin\frac{C}{2}-3$
$=-4\sin^2\frac{C}{2}+2\sin\frac{C}{2}+1$
$t=\sin\frac{C}{2}$
即有$\frac{1}{a}=-4t^2+2t+1=-4(t-\frac{1}{4})^2+\frac{5}{4}\le\frac{5}{4}$
$\therefore a\ge\frac{4}{5}$当$\sin\frac{C}{2}=\frac{1}{4}$时$\text{“=”}$成立
