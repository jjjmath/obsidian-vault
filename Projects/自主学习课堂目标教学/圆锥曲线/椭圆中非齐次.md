已知椭圆的方程为$\frac{x^2}{4}+\frac{y^2}{3}=1$,过右焦点$F$的直线与椭圆交于点$A,B$,点$P$在直线$l:x=4$上，且$AP$与直线$l$垂直，
(1)求证直线$PB$过定点
(2)若三角形$POB$的面积为$S$,求$S$的取值范围
【解析】
![[20261104001]]
设直线$AB$的方程为$x=ty+1,A(x_1,y_1),B(x_2,y_2)$
点$P(4,y_1)$
联立$$\begin{cases}x=ty+1\\3x^2+4y^2-12=0\end{cases}$$整理得$$(3t^2+4)y^2+6ty-9=0$$
$$y_1+y_2=-\frac{-6t}{3t^2+4},y_1y_2=\frac{-9}{3t^2+4}$$
直线$BP$得斜率$$k=\frac{y_2-y_1}{x_2-4}$$
<span style='color:red'>因为</span>$\color{red}x_2$<span style='color:red'>的存在，导致斜率形式不整齐，所以先对斜率进行整理</span>
$$y_1+y_2=-\frac{-6t}{3t^2+4},ty_1y_2=\frac{-9t}{3t^2+4}$$
所以有$$\frac{ty_1y_2}{y_1+y_2}=\frac{3}{2}$$
$$k=\frac{y_2-y_1}{x_2-4}=\frac{y_2-y_1}{ty_2-3}=\frac{y_2-y_1}{ty_2-\frac{2ty_1y_2}{y_1+y_2}}=\frac{y_1+y_2}{ty_2}=\frac{\frac{2}{3}ty_1y_2}{ty_2}=\frac{2}{3}y_1$$
所以直线$BP$的方程为$$y-y_1=\frac{2y_1}{3}(x-4)$$
整理可得$$y=\frac{2y_1}{3}x-\frac{8y_1}{3}+y_1=\frac{2y_2}{3}(x-\frac{5}{2})$$
所以直线$BP$过定点$(-\frac{5}{2},0)$


<span style='color:blue'>另一种写法</span>
直线$BP$的方程$$y-y_1=\frac{y_2-y_1}{x_2-4}(x-4)=\frac{y_2-y_1}{ty_2-3}x+\frac{4(y_1-y_2)+ty_1y_2-3y_1}{ty_2-3}$$
$$y=\frac{y_2-y_1}{ty_2-3}x+\frac{4(y_1-y_2)+\frac{3}{2}(y_1+y_2)-3y_1}{ty_2-3}=\frac{y_2-y_1}{ty_2-3}(x-\frac{5}{2})$$
所以直线$BP$过定点$(-\frac{5}{2},0)$
