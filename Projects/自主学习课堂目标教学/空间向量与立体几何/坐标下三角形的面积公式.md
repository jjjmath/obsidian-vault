1.已知三点$O(0,0),A(x_1,y_1),B(x_2,y_2)$,则$S_{\triangle AOB}=\frac{1}{2}|x_1y_2-x_2y_1|$
2.更一般化
$A(x_1,y_1),B(x_2,y_2),C(x_3,y_3)$则$S_{\triangle ABC}=\frac{1}{2}|(x_2-x_1)(y_3-y_1)-(x_3-x_1)(y_2-y_1)|$
更对称的表达式$S_{\triangle ABC}=\frac{1}{2}|x_2(y_3-y_1)+x_2(y_3-y_1)+x_3(y_1-y_2)|$
3.【空间坐标下三角形面积】
- $\vec{a}=(x_1,y_1,z_1),\vec{b}=(x_2,y_2,z_2)$
- $\vec{a}\times\vec{b}=(y_1z_2-z_1y_2,z_2x_1-z_1x_2,x_1y_2-x_2y_1)$
- $|\vec{a}\times\vec{b}|=|\vec{a}||\vec{b}|\sin\theta,\theta$为$\vec{a},\vec{b}$的夹角
- 空间三角形的面积$S=\frac{1}{2}\sqrt{(y_1z_2-z_1y_2)^2+(z_2x_1-z_1x_2)^2+(x_1y_2-x_2y_1)^2}$
